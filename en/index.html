About Me

More than just keeping the infrastructure running, my focus is on building secure, automated environments designed to support your business growth and continuity.

For over 15 years, I've been building my career in IT infrastructure. Starting with traditional server administration, I have evolved into leading teams and strategic projects across cloud and hybrid environments. This solid foundation enables me to translate operational needs into smart decisions regarding architecture, security, and high availability.

My day-to-day work revolves around guiding the transition from legacy environments to modern, scalable infrastructures. By leveraging DevOps practices and Infrastructure as Code (IaC), my goal is clear: to standardize configurations, automate repetitive tasks, and reduce manual intervention. This results in faster, more agile deliveries and changes that are fully predictable and traceable.

For all of this to run flawlessly, security must be the foundation. I have a strong background in Identity and Access Management (IAM), ensuring the protection of complex environments. This expertise is what ties everything together, connecting servers, networks, cloud, and governance into a single, integrated vision of IT.
